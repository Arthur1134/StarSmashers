HOW TO PLAY

CONTROLS

Choose in Settings. Rotate + Thrust: A/D turn, W thrust, S reverse, Space/click fire forward, screen wrap. WASD + Mouse: move any direction, mouse aim, click fire. Esc pause.

SURVIVE & LEVEL UP

Kill enemies for XP. Level-ups offer upgrades — rarer picks (pink/yellow) define your build. Bosses drop stars for special upgrades.

SCRAP ECONOMY

Enemies drop scrap & crates. Collect floating debris. Pause and open SCRAP SHOP to spend scrap on mid-run boosts (heal, damage, XP, shield).

OBJECTIVES

Clear timed missions (survive, collect scrap, kill elites, clear swarms) for scrap bonuses or bonus upgrade chests.

EVENTS

Watch for banners: asteroid storms, pirate raids, supply drops, and more. Adapt quickly.

META

Earn credits between runs for permanent store upgrades. Unlock ships via achievements.
